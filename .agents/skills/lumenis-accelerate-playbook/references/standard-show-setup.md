# Standard show setup

The 2027 shows start from the same formula as the Nashville and Scottsdale shows, then change only what a venue or audience requires. Record every value in the event's **Fast Facts** sheet (or the sheet the event folder uses for logistics), not in this repository.

## E1 Room map (T-90)

Copy the last show's room plan: main stage, breakout rooms, demo rooms and the service success station, for Vision and Aesthetics. Note any room the venue can't match and propose the nearest alternative. Which division takes the main stage is decided later (C9) from attendee numbers; plan rooms so either can.

## E2 Device list (T-90)

The devices brought to every show are standard. Copy the last show's list and ask the clinical device coordinator to confirm any change. Clinical owns its own supplies, iPads and Jotform consent forms; marketing only prints the backup paper consent forms (print kit).

## E13 Arrival, huddles and success station (T-7)

- Staff arrive at **1 PM** on the Friday.
- **Joint sales huddle first, 1:30-2 PM,** in a breakout room; then separate Vision and Aesthetics huddles in other rooms. Confirm with the venue's event lead that the room will be ready by then. A small venue may force a game-day change.
- **Service success station:** tabletop or a formal spot, depending on the venue; the service team brings tablecloths and banners. Location can be a game-day call; record the plan anyway.

## E14 Registration desk (T-7)

Usually staffed by back office (asked for at E4). Training covers:

1. OneTap check-in on their phone.
2. The self-registration QR code (reuse the last show's template) for walk-ups.
3. The handheld Bluetooth badge printers: each printer pairs with a staff phone; staff type the name into the printer app and stick the label on a blank badge.
4. A test print on site before doors open.

Hold the training as a short call when anyone on the desk is new.
