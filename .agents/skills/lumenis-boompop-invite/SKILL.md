# BoomPop staff invites

Owner: Kaelyn (event logistics).
Status: draft procedure, written 2026-10-29 from the Vision Accelerate planning session. Kaelyn built a BoomPop agent at the first AI workshop; merge its tested steps into this file before first use. Review required.

BoomPop is where Lumenis employees (sales, marketing, back office, service, clinical) register for an Accelerate. It is separate from prospect registration, which runs through the rep approval and flight sheets in Smartsheet. Each teammate has their own BoomPop admin login on the team plan, so nobody depends on one person's password.

## Inputs

- The show's Key Info: city, dates, venue, hotel booking link (E3), staff arrival time.
- The last Accelerate's BoomPop page (the copy source).
- Department leaders for sales, marketing, back office and service, and who leads each division's sales team for this show.
- Browser access to BoomPop, signed in by the person running this. Never ask for or type a password.

## Procedure

### E4 Ask leaders for invitees (T-70)

Draft one email per leader: show city and dates, a reply-by date seven days out, and a request for the names (and roles) of everyone they want there. Ask back office separately who will staff the registration desk. Ask early: people need time to plan, and replies take a while.

### E5 Build the page and invite (T-60)

1. **Duplicate** the last show's BoomPop page. Never edit the old page.
2. **Fill it from Smartsheet,** not from the old page: city, dates, venue and address, hotel booking link, arrival time (1 PM Friday), and the show's schedule outline. Check every field that still names the old city or dates.
3. **Read the page back** in preview: dates, links and venue all match Key Info.
4. **Load the head count** from the leaders' replies and send invitations to everyone at once. People added later get the invite and link one-off.
5. Record the page link and head count in Key Info.

### E6 Track RSVPs (T-60 to T-7)

Weekly: list who hasn't responded and draft one reminder each. For every "no", draft a note to that person's leader asking whether they want to nominate someone else. Stop when there are no open RSVPs.

### E16 Staff Know Before You Go (T-7)

One email to everyone who said yes: hotel and booking link, arrival at 1 PM Friday, huddle times and rooms (from E13), dress, what to bring, and the on-site marketing contacts.

## Boundaries

- Drafts only: the owner sends invitations, reminders and emails.
- Don't publish a BoomPop page with an old city, date or link on it.
- Attendee names and RSVP lists stay in BoomPop and Smartsheet, never in this repository.
