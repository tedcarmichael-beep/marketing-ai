# Accelerate KOL messages

Step IDs match the Vision *TEMPLATE - Accelerate* sheet. Brackets are filled from the show's Key Info. Session briefings (who presents what, when, with deck links) are a separate agent: [lumenis-accelerate-kbyg-emails](../../lumenis-accelerate-kbyg-emails/SKILL.md).

Recurring brackets:

- **[honorarium]:** the KOL's amount in the show record (K2 Key Info). The usual Accelerate honorarium is $3,500, and some KOLs have agreed a higher amount; always use the record. No amount blocks the invite.
- **[T&E]:** the travel and expense allowance in the record. The usual amount is $1,500 and it is the same for every KOL.
- **[arrival] / [departure]:** the times the KOL must be at, and may leave, the **hotel**: usually 7:00 PM on the Friday and any time after 7:00 PM Saturday. Always say these are hotel times, not flight times. KOLs present nothing on Sunday unless the record says so.
- **[hotel link]:** the room-block booking link (E3). Optional line when it isn't ready yet.

|---|---|---|---|
| K3 | Invitation | T-90 | Nicholas |

## K3 Invitation

**Subject:** Invitation to present at Lumenis Accelerate [city], [dates]

Hi Dr. [Last name],

We'd be honored to have you share your expertise with your colleagues on the Lumenis stage at Accelerate [city], [dates], at [venue], [city, state].

Here's what we'd ask:

- **Be at the hotel by [arrival] on [Friday date].** You're free to leave any time after [departure] on [Saturday date]. These are times to be at and leave the hotel, so please plan flights that get you there comfortably before [arrival].
- **Present your sessions on [Saturday date].** Once you accept, we'll send the exact sessions and go over them with you.

We offer a [honorarium] honorarium plus [T&E] for travel and expenses.

If you're able to join us, just reply here and we'll follow up with the details.

Thank you for considering it,
Nicholas

*Notes:* send about three months out. Don't list sessions here. Don't offer to pay for staff or guests; handle those requests one-off.

## K4a Follow-up, returning KOL, no new deck

**Subject:** Re: Invitation to present at Lumenis Accelerate [city], [dates]

Hi Dr. [Last name],

Excellent, thank you, and we're really looking forward to working with you again.

I'll be in touch in the coming weeks with your schedule and a link to all of your decks.

Lumenis finds it easiest for you to book your own travel. Please keep your receipts for your final invoice; they're covered within your [T&E] travel and expense budget. [Optional: You can book your room at our discounted rate here: [hotel link].]

Best,
Ted

*Notes:* use when the KOL has presented at an Accelerate before and isn't presenting a deck that's new to them. Don't offer a meeting; it wastes their time and ours.

## K4b Follow-up, new KOL or new deck

**Subject:** Re: Invitation to present at Lumenis Accelerate [city], [dates]

Hi Dr. [Last name],

Excellent, thank you. I'm really looking forward to working with you on this.

I'll be in touch in the coming weeks to set up a time to go over the sessions we have planned for you[ and to learn your story, so we can weave your experience and results into the deck].

Lumenis finds it easiest for you to book your own travel. Please keep your receipts for your final invoice; they're covered within your [T&E] travel and expense budget. [Optional: You can book your room at our discounted rate here: [hotel link].]

Best,
Ted

*Notes:* include the "learn your story" clause for a KOL new to Accelerate; drop it when only the deck is new.
