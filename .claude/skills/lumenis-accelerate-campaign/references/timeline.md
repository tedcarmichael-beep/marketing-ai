## Rules agreed with the timeline

- **The promo page is Learn More, not registration.** Visitors tell their rep they're interested; reps register prospects through the rep approval process.
- **Smartsheet is the source of truth for attendance.** Em2 and Em3 lists come from Smartsheet and OneTap via [attendee lists](../../lumenis-accelerate-attendee-lists/SKILL.md), never from HubSpot form fills.
- **One launch day.** Nothing public goes live early or piecemeal.
- **Test leads use ZIP `00000`** so they route to marketing operations, not a rep.
- **Workflow clone source** for Vision: the most recent Vision Accelerate interest workflow (as of 2026-10-29, the Q4 2026 Miami one).
