# Event photo selection rubric

Status: draft, 2026-10-27. Calibrated on 57 photos marketing had already chosen for the Dropbox `/Selected Images` library from four Accelerate events. Each pick was compared with the frames shot right before and after it, plus a random sample of photos that weren't chosen. The rules were then corrected over five rounds of feedback on two new events (a trade show and an Accelerate summit).

## What the library is for

`/Selected Images` is a **brand image library**, not a "best of" album for each event. Marketing reuses these photos for web, email, social and decks: Lumenis devices, branding, venues, speakers and people enjoying the event. Choose photos someone could drop into a landing page, not a record of who attended.

## Target

- **About 3–6% of each sub-shoot** (booth, dinner, general session): roughly 10–20 picks per event. The two events used to correct this rubric ended at 19 and 17 picks from 593 photos. Leaving good photos out is normal.
- **About 70–75% people.** Every event still gets about 4–5 strong photos without people (device hero, brand signage, styled detail, the set or venue). Never zero.
- Cover several categories. Don't take 10 shots of one thing.

## People photos: expression comes first

- Pick faces that look **fun, warm, inviting and flattering**: real smiles, laughter, playful props, hugs, toasts.
- Reject neutral, distracted, mid-word, squinting or awkward faces, even when the composition or branding is strong.
- **Posed lineups** (people in a row smiling at the camera against a plain wall) are fine in small doses, at most one or two per event. Prefer people *doing something* (demos, laughing, toasting, playing with props), or a pose in front of something distinctive (branded backdrop, sign, device).
- **Speaker and panel shots with energy are keepers**: a presenter mid-gesture, a co-panelist reacting, a branded stage wall behind.
- **Treatment and demo shots** need the device, the handpiece and the patient visible, and they only qualify if the clinician's expression is good (smiling or warmly engaged). Clinical focus alone isn't enough.

## Photos without people

- **Must read instantly**: one clear subject (a device, a sign, a styled detail, an empty stage) on a clean background.
- Reject busy, static scenes where the viewer has to hunt for what the photo is of, such as a wide shot of a cluttered booth.
- Atmosphere counts when it is clean and distinctive: a styled lounge, an architectural space, the set before doors open.
- **Product and display shots must show Lumenis products only.** Skip tables of partner or third-party products, even at a Lumenis event.

## Categories, in priority order

1. Treatment or demo in action, with a good clinician expression.
2. Brand moments: logos, signage, anniversary marks, branded chairs and stage walls, shot tight and clean.
3. Speakers and panels on a branded stage, animated.
4. People enjoying the event: warm candids, props, toasts, staff with branding behind them.
5. Venue and set: the stage before doors open, a styled lounge, a distinctive space.
6. Styled details: florals, menus, branded table settings and print.
7. Device hero shot: the product alone on clean ground.

## Hard rejects

- Eyes closed or half-blinked on a prominent face.
- Mid-word, grimacing or caught mid-motion.
- Backs of audience heads in the foreground of a stage shot.
- Audience-only "watching" or reaction shots.
- Cluttered wide shots with no clear subject.
- Out of focus, motion blur, or blown or crushed exposure on the subject.
- Non-Lumenis products as the subject.
- Close-ups of identifiable attendees with no event or brand context. Prefer staff, speakers and KOLs.

## Tie-breakers between near-identical frames

- The better expression.
- Energy over stiffness: a playful pose or gesture over a static stance.
- The tighter frame where the subject fills it cleanly, unless the wider frame adds the brand sign.
- More brand or slide content visible.
- Clean negative space that can take a text overlay.

## Composition and technical checks

- The subject is on a rule-of-thirds line, or deliberately centered on a symmetrical set.
- The background is uncluttered. A branded wall counts as clean.
- The subject is sharp and properly exposed. Warm or dramatic light is fine.
- Landscape and portrait are both acceptable.

## Open questions

- Should photos whose main subjects are identifiable attendees enter the shared HubSpot catalog, or only staff, speakers and KOLs? Until the subject owner decides, these may go to Selected Images but are marked **catalog pending decision** and are not uploaded or cataloged.
- Is the same rubric right for every event type and for Aesthetics? It was calibrated on Vision Accelerate events and corrected on one trade show.
