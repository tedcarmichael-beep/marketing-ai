# General photo selection guidance

Status: draft, proposed 2026-10-27 to support Nicholas's request for a reusable HubSpot image repository. Non-event guidance has not been tested on a live collection. Ted is the proposed subject reviewer.

The source folders are the required selection input. A future campaign, placement or use is not required. Preserve varied, useful library candidates and describe them accurately so AI can choose later.

- Prefer sharp, properly exposed images with a clear subject and readable composition. Inspect full-resolution shortlisted files rather than relying only on contact sheets.
- Remove redundant burst picks; retain genuinely distinct views, orientations, backgrounds and compositions. Do not apply a fixed people quota or event yield to other folders.
- Product photos: prioritize accurate, identifiable devices and useful distinct angles/details; do not guess the model or product from a filename.
- Headshots: prioritize clear, flattering faces and clean backgrounds; do not require event branding or activity.
- Facilities and environments: prioritize clear spaces, distinctive features and usable compositions; people are optional.
- Other collections: apply clarity, technical quality, variety and duplicate control. State uncertainty rather than inventing a specialized rubric or demanding a future use.
- For event shoots, use the calibrated [event selection rubric](selection-rubric.md), including its attendee hold. Its ratios, category priorities and event-context rules apply only to that scope.

Record visible people, screens, patient information, third-party branding and other restrictions for owner review. Do not infer image rights from selection quality or from a person's role. Follow the image-library rules for clearance, metadata and publication.
